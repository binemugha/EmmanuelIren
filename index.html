About Emmanuel

Teacher, church planter, and cultural voice.

Emmanuel founded Celebration Church International (CCI) to raise believers who know exactly what Christ has done and express that conviction in every sphere. Today CCI spans multiple campuses across Africa, Europe, and North America while keeping discipleship and evangelism at the center.

Beyond the pulpit, he writes, curates music, and mentors ministry teams—equipping leaders to teach Christ with accuracy and creativity.
