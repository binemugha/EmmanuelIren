# Emmanuel Iren Website — Project Brief

## 1. Brand Overview

### 1.1 Brand Identity
- **Name**: Apostle Emmanuel Iren
- **Domain**: emmanueliren.com
- **Symbol**: π (Pi)
- **Brand Promise**: Raising an army of culture influencers by apostolic ministry and role modelling in music, movies, and media.

#### The π Identity System
- **P** — Pastor: His calling. His office. The first word of his identity.
- **I** — Iren: His name. His heritage. Akwa Ibom to the world.
- **E** — Emmanuel: God with us. The name of his God and his mission.
- **3.14** — John 3:14: "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of Man be lifted up: that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life."
- **π** — Mathematically: Infinite. Irrational. Never fully contained. A God who cannot be boxed — a ministry that will not be stopped.

### 1.2 Brand Tone
Authoritative. Warm. Sharp. Joyful. Culturally fluent. The site should feel like a cross between a world-class pastor's personal brand and a culture leader's platform — weighty and warm in equal measure. Not a church website. A personal brand site for one of Nigeria's most significant apostolic voices.

### 1.3 Target Audience
- Young Nigerian professionals and graduates seeking theological depth
- Pastors and ministry leaders
- The global African diaspora
- Culture-watchers who respect the intersection of excellence and gospel

## 2. Design System

### 2.1 Colour Palette
| Swatch | Name | Hex | Role |
|--------|------|-----|------|
| Black | #080810 | Primary background — all pages |
| Deep Navy | #0D0D1A | Card surfaces, nav, stats bar |
| Gold | #C4973A | PRIMARY ACCENT — π logo, headings, CTAs, borders, labels |
| Cream | #F2EDE4 | Primary text colour |
| Muted Grey | #8A8A9A | Secondary text, captions |
| Red (Signal Only) | #C0392B | Live badges, New badges, Watch Live CTA only. NEVER decorative. |

### 2.2 Typography
| Role | Font | Weight |
|------|------|--------|
| Display / Headings | Cormorant Garamond | 300, 400, Italic |
| Body / UI | DM Sans | 300, 400, 500 |

Google Fonts: Load Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans

### 2.3 CSS Variables
```css
--black: #080810
--navy: #0D0D1A
--navy2: #111122
--gold: #C4973A
--gold-lt: #E2B96A
--red: #C0392B
--cream: #F2EDE4
--muted: #8A8A9A
--serif: 'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif
--sans: 'DM Sans', sans-serif
```

### 2.4 Design Principles
- Gold dominates. It is the only brand accent. Red is functional only — never decorative.
- π on every page. In the nav logo (small, gold) and as a watermark in the hero (large, near-transparent).
- Typography is non-negotiable. Cormorant Garamond for display, DM Sans for UI. Do not substitute.
- Dark background only. Dark mode site. No light mode.
- Mobile-first. His audience is primarily on mobile.
- One primary CTA per page. No cluttered actions above the fold.

## 6. Red Accent — Implementation Rules

Red (#C0392B) is approved as a functional signal colour only. It must NEVER appear in brand identity, scripture, typography, or navigation as decoration.

| Element | Approved? | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Live Now nav pill | ✓ Yes | Only when actively streaming |
| Watch Live CTA (hero) | ✓ Yes | Only when live — replaces standard CTA |
| Live banner on sermon video | ✓ Yes | Only during live service |
| New product badges | ✓ Yes | Store only |
| Typography / body text | ✗ Never | Gold handles all emphasis |
| π logo / scripture sections | ✗ Never | Brand identity zones are gold only |
| Navigation active state | ✗ Never | Active nav = gold |
| Section backgrounds | ✗ Never | All backgrounds are black/navy |
