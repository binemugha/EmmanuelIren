About Emmanuel Iren

Apostle Emmanuel Iren is the visionary founder and Lead Pastor of Celebration Church International (CCI), a fast-growing global ministry with over 15,000 members and more than 30 campuses in Nigeria, the UK, Canada, and the USA. He is renowned for his dynamic preaching, teaching, and gospel songwriting. His mission is to help people discover and confidently live out their God-given purpose through the transformative power of the Gospel.

In addition to leading CCI, he oversees two significant platforms: Manifest Bible Course, an interdenominational Bible training program, and the Triumph30 Devotional, a daily devotional designed to help believers develop a consistent prayer life and relationship with God. Both platforms are instrumental in equipping believers to live purpose-driven lives rooted in biblical truth.
